Privacy for PDF MARIN on the Mac App Store

Opening, annotating, and saving a PDF normally take place on your Mac. PDF compression and adding or removing password protection also run on your Mac.

When you submit a request from Ask the developer, the app sends the category and the message you entered. If you ask for a reply and enter a reply address, the app also sends that address. If you choose to attach an image, screenshot, PDF, or another file, the attachment is sent with the request. If you choose to include diagnostic context, the app also sends information such as the app and operating system versions. We use this information to receive feedback, answer support requests, and investigate problems.

Reports submitted through the in-app bug report and feature request form include the app and OS versions, processor architecture, runtime versions, and the size of the app’s content area. If a document is open, its page count is included. A name entered in the optional field is included in the report text.

For in-app replies, we use a reference number for each request and an identifier to associate requests from the same app profile. To receive reports, respond, and investigate issues, we may use an email delivery service to notify the developer, including the report text, any reply address you entered, attachments you selected, and diagnostic information. We process the connection’s IP address at intake to limit repeated submissions in a short period.

If you choose in-app replies, the app uses a support reference and a saved record to retrieve replies. When secure storage is available, that record is encrypted on your Mac. You can check replies manually. If automatic reply checking is enabled and you have a saved request, the app also checks for replies. You can turn off automatic checking in the inquiries window.

The website report form opens in your browser. A report you submit there follows a separate route from an in-app conversation. Your report and any attachments you choose to send may pass through the support and email delivery services. The website privacy policy describes the website’s data practices.

If you use a web URL to load shared text snippets, the app requests the list of snippets from that address. This request does not contain open PDFs or the text of your saved snippets. The destination may process information such as your IP address to handle the connection.

We handle support conversations and attachments as needed to receive reports, reply, and investigate issues. Notification emails, forwarded copies, and reports sent through the website are handled according to the separate processes for those channels.

To request access, correction, restriction, or deletion, or to withdraw consent, contact us through the website report form. Provide only the details needed to identify the request. Turning off automatic reply checks does not delete any data you already sent.
